Strategic priorities
Threads first, everything else repurposed

Threads is your best engagement-to-follower ratio. Every episode generates three posts: a hook before release, a clip moment after, and a conversation prompt 48 hours later. Use those posts to actively drive Spotify and Apple follows.

TikTok is your fastest path to new ears

Short garage clips — 30 to 60 seconds of the best moment from each guest interview — have the highest discovery ceiling. Post within 24 hours of recording. The goal isn't TikTok followers; it's Spotify and Apple listeners who find you there.

Apple Podcasts is quietly your biggest audience

Apple drives roughly 25–35% of podcast consumption and skews older, which matches your TC listener profile. Make sure your show art and description are fully optimized there — it costs nothing and moves the needle on the 1K listener milestone.

First sponsor: 90 days out, pitch direct

At ~500 combined audience with consistent weekly output, pitch specialty coffee, car care brands (Griot's, Chemical Guys), or tristate performance shops directly. No middleman needed at this stage — a one-page media kit is enough.
